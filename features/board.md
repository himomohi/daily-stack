# Board placement

- 4 x 4 grid, 6 cm cells, thin light plate with grid lines.
- On XR start and on Recenter: 0.5 m in front of the head, 0.4 m below eye level (clamped 0.6 to 1.3 m), yawed to face the user.
- Tray 13 cm in front of the board edge; buttons between tray and board. Everything sits inside about a 0.6 m radius (seated, 2 ft rule).
